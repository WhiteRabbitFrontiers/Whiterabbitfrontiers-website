The Writable Brain.

The frontier between observing neural activity and influencing it is becoming more precise. But what does it mean to make a brain signal addressable?

RESEARCH BRIEF

This is an editorial explainer introducing an investigation, not a claim that speculative capabilities have been demonstrated. Last editorial update: October 24, 2026.

Reading is not writing

EEG and other neural measurements can reveal patterns of activity. Brain-computer interfaces may translate selected signals into commands. Neither implies that a system can read thoughts in full or precisely rewrite a person's experiences.

Targeted intervention

Optogenetics uses light-sensitive proteins to influence selected cells, mainly in experimental settings. Deep brain stimulation and other clinical interventions work through different mechanisms and have specific indications and limitations.

The hard questions

Specificity, safety, consent and reversibility matter as much as technical capability. White Rabbit Frontiers is watching the evidence for better targeting, more useful interfaces and safeguards against overclaiming.

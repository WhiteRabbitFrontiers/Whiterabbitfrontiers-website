## The three selected investigations (in user-selected order)

### 1. What If AI Made Everyone More Valuable?
- **Frontier:** Human Possibility / AI & autonomy
- **Editorial question:** What if organizations optimized for human capabilities, invention and opportunity instead of workforce reduction alone?
- **Development:** Verify independent workplace case studies, workforce outcomes, pay, training, and counterexamples. Distinguish productivity gains from career development.
- **Existing brief:** `radar/rdq-what-if-ai-made-everyone-more-valuable.md`

### 2. Cancer Is Losing Its Address
- **Frontier:** Longevity & biotech / precision oncology
- **Editorial question:** How are molecular and genomic characteristics changing cancer diagnosis and treatment across traditional organ-based categories?
- **Development:** Compare tissue-agnostic drug approvals with organ-specific treatment; report examples, eligibility, response rates, resistance, cost and access. Obtain primary trial and regulatory sources. Do not imply all cancer care has moved beyond organ of origin.

### 3. ER-100: Reprogramming Aging Nerve Cells
- **Frontier:** Longevity & biotech / regenerative neurobiology
- **Editorial question:** Can controllable epigenetic reprogramming repair damaged retinal nerve cells in humans?
- **Development:** Trace trial registry and protocol, safety follow-up, independent expert perspectives, visual-field endpoint interpretation and biological age evidence. Distinguish company-reported preliminary safety from proven rejuvenation or efficacy.
- **Existing research note:** `radar/er-100-epigenetic-reprogramming.md`

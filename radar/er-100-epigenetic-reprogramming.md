# ER-100: Can We Reprogram Aging Nerve Cells?

**White Rabbit Radar | Featured | October 23, 2026**  
**Category:** Programmable Self / Neural & Biological Repair  
**Evidence status:** EARLY HUMAN TRIAL · PHASE 1 · COMPANY-REPORTED  
**White Rabbit File:** No. Cellular state is not personal identity.

## The signal

For the first time, an experimental epigenetic-reprogramming therapy has produced an initial safety readout in people with glaucoma. Life Biosciences reported October 8 that the first three recipients of ER-100 tolerated treatment through day 56 without serious adverse events or dose-limiting toxicities. Two showed preliminary improvements at some visual-field test locations.

## How it works

ER-100 delivers genes encoding three transcription factors, OCT4, SOX2 and KLF4 (OSK), into retinal cells through a single injection into the eye. Daily doxycycline activates the introduced genes for eight weeks. Researchers hope to shift damaged retinal ganglion cells toward more youthful, functional gene-expression patterns.

**Gene delivery → externally induced OSK expression → attempted cellular-state change → visual-function measurement.**

## Why we're watching

Most glaucoma treatments seek to slow further injury. ER-100 explores a more radical possibility: changing the internal biological program of surviving neurons. It is a genuine step toward programmable cellular states, but it is **not** mind-controlled gene editing, and it does not show that human neurons have been rejuvenated.

## What the results do and don't say

The company reported no serious adverse events among three participants over 56 days and preliminary visual-field changes in two. At least 28% of tested *locations* improved by more than 4 dB in each of those two participants; **this does not mean their overall vision improved 28%.**

The study is small, open-label and uncontrolled. Visual-field testing can vary. Long-term safety, true biological rejuvenation, durable functional recovery and clinical efficacy remain unproven. The safety board permitted testing a higher dose, not approval for routine treatment.

## White Rabbit verdict

**Major frontier breadcrumb, not a proven therapy.** Human testing has begun for a controllable gene-expression intervention aimed at repairing damaged neural function.

**Follow next:** Higher-dose safety, longer-term follow-up, controlled efficacy trials, direct evidence of epigenetic age reversal, and durability after doxycycline stops.

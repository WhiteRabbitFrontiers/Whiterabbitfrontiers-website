**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / P1. Not approved for publication. Updated 2026-10-26.

## Primary and high-authority sources
- National Cancer Institute tissue-agnostic therapies overview: biomarker-defined indications across tumor locations, while site of origin continues to matter for much oncology. https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/types/agnostic-cancer-therapies-hp-pdq
- FDA oncology approvals listing: **October 16, 2026** traditional approval for pembrolizumab (and subcutaneous formulation) for a specified advanced TMB-high indication, with revised threshold and other eligibility constraints. Check the FDA announcement, updated label and companion diagnostics before writing numerical claims. https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/oncology-cancerhematologic-malignancies-approval-notifications
- FDA guidance on tissue-agnostic development: a molecular marker across multiple tumor types is the basis, not evidence that anatomical context disappears. https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/tissue-agnostic-drug-development-oncology
- AACR October 5, 2026 regulatory review notes traditional approval of selpercatinib for RET fusion-positive solid tumors. Confirm complete indication and evidence with FDA prescribing information. https://www.aacr.org/blog/2026/10/05/fda-approvals-in-oncology-july-september-2026/

## Outstanding reporting
1. Build treatment table: molecular target, drug, FDA indication, date, prior therapy, eligible ages, confirmatory requirement.
2. Pull pivotal trial protocols/publications and report denominators, objective response, response durability, toxicity and caveats.
3. Show where the same target responds differently by cancer site, plus resistance and treatment sequencing.
4. Find documented access barriers: genomic testing, reimbursement, geography and out-of-pocket costs.
5. Obtain independent oncologist assessment and responsibly documented patient example.

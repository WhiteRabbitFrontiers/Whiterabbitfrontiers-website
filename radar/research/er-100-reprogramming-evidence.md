## Source inventory
- Clinical trial record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07290244 . Verify current registry version, primary safety endpoints, visits, eligibility and protocol changes.
- Sponsor's June 9, 2026 announcement of first dosing: https://www.lifebiosciences.com/life-biosciences-announces-first-patient-dosed-in-phase-1-trial-of-er-100-for-optic-neuropathies/
- Sponsor's October 8, 2026 interim announcement: three glaucoma participants followed through Day 56, no reported serious adverse events or dose-limiting toxicities; in two, >=28% of sampled *visual-field locations* had >4dB improvement. **Not 28% overall vision recovery, not a controlled efficacy finding, not measured reversal of human biological age.** https://www.lifebiosciences.com/life-biosciences-announces-first-in-human-data-from-ongoing-phase-1-trial-evaluating-er-100-in-optic-neuropathies/
- Preclinical scientific background: Lu et al., Nature (2020), reprogramming and visual function in animal models. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2975-4

## Outstanding reporting
1. Retrieve trial protocol and registry change history; distinguish Phase 1 safety from exploratory functional endpoints.
2. Seek presentation slides / numerical participant-level plots and baseline versus repeat testing.
3. Ask independent glaucoma/neuro-ophthalmology specialists about learning effects, variability and clinical significance of Humphrey field findings.
4. Investigate long-term potential safety issues, including uncontrolled reprogramming and gene-delivery inflammation.
5. Find actual human epigenetic-age measurements, if any, rather than assuming rejuvenation from the proposed mechanism.
6. Track dose escalation and longer follow-up, including durability after doxycycline ends.

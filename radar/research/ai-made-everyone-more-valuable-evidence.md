# What If AI Made Everyone More Valuable? | Evidence desk
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT / P1. Not approved for publication. Updated 2026-10-26.
**Existing brief:** radar/rdq-what-if-ai-made-everyone-more-valuable.md

## Verified leads (not a completed fact-check)
- 2023 Ingka Group statement: Billie resolved ~47% of incoming inquiries (2021–23), ~3.2 million interactions, ~EUR 13 million reported savings; company says 8,500 call-center staff were reskilled. **Company-reported; not verified retention, wages, promotions or counterfactual headcount.** https://www.ingka.com/newsroom/ai-and-remote-selling-bring-ikea-design-expertise-to-the-many/
- Forrester analysis by Betsy Summers, July 10, 2026: a newer secondary account of IKEA. Investigate revenue framing, timeframe, sourcing, and whether incremental revenue is causally attributable to retraining. https://www.forrester.com/blogs/ai-helped-ikea-create-e1-3-billion-in-new-revenue-but-not-how-you-think/
- Peer-reviewed customer-support study: Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond (2025), QJE; existing brief reports 5,172 agents and ~15% productivity lift, especially for less-experienced workers. Check publication tables and learning outcomes. https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044
- Noy & Zhang, Science (2023), randomized professional writing tasks; task improvements are not demonstrated wage effects. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adh2586
- Dillon et al. NBER working paper 33795 (2025); workplace tool adoption does not by itself prove organizational redesign. https://www.nber.org/papers/w33795
